## GLOSSARY

**Equity Beta (3y):** Beta is a measure of the tendency of securities to move with the market as a whole. A beta of 1 indicates that the security's price will move with the market. A beta less than 1 indicates the security tends to be less volatile than the market, while a beta greater than 1 indicates the security is more volatile than the market. Calculated vs. S&amp;P 500.

**Benchmark Level:** The CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate – New York Variant is a once a day (4pm ET) benchmark price for bitcoin, measured in US dollars per bitcoin.

**Basket Amount:** The Basket Amount for a given day is calculated by multiplying the NAV by the number of shares in each Basket (40,000).

**Standard Deviation (3y):** Standard deviation measures how dispersed returns are around the average. A higher standard deviation indicates that returns are spread out over a larger range of values and thus, more volatile.

**Distribution Frequency:** How often a distribution is paid by the fund. The most common distribution frequencies are annually, biannually and quarterly.

**Indicative Basket Bitcoin Amount:** The Indicative Basket Bitcoin Amount for a given day is calculated by multiplying the next day's projected NAV by the number of shares in each Basket (40,000), divided by that day's CF Benchmarks Index (CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate – New York Variant).

**Basket Bitcoin Amount:** The Basket Bitcoin Amount for a given day is calculated by dividing the Basket Amount for that day by that day's CF Benchmarks Index (CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate – New York Variant).

**Shares Outstanding:** Shares outstanding reflect the positions as of the prior day, in accordance with the accounting book of records.

**Number of Holdings:** The number of holdings in the fund excluding cash positions and derivatives such as futures and currency forwards.

**Net Assets of Fund:** On each Business Day, the Trust administrator evaluates the bitcoin held by the Trust based on the Index, unless the Sponsor in its sole discretion determines that the Index is unreliable, and determines the net asset value of the Trust by taking the total assets of the Trust, which consists solely of bitcoin and cash, less total liabilities of the Trust. The net asset value of the Trust reflects the position as of the prior day in accordance with the accounting books of record.
