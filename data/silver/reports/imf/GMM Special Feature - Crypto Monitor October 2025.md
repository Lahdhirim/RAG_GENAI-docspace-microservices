## Market Update

**Total crypto assets market cap surpassed USD 4.2 trillion.**

**Bitcoin lost 4 percent market share of total crypto assets to Ether.**

### 2. Selected Crypto Assets Market Share (in percent)

- The total crypto assets market cap surpassed USD 4.2 trillion, setting a new historical high. This represents a 13 percent increase from the previous high of USD 3.9 trillion in December 2024.
- Similarly, the Bitcoin market cap increased by 9 percent in Q3 2025, hitting USD 2.5 trillion for the first time. Bitcoin price reached USD 126,000 on October 6th.
- While Bitcoin's share in crypto assets was close to its peak at the beginning of Q3 2025, it dropped by 4 percent to 56 percent of the total market share by the end of the quarter.
- Instead, Ether's share in crypto assets rose by 4 percent during Q3 2025 to 13 percent.
- Other crypto assets represent 30 percent of the crypto market share and have generally been decreasing since 2023 (by 15 percentage points from their historical highs).

- Chainalysis constructs its crypto adoption index by estimating transaction volumes across centralized and decentralized services, then weighing them by each country's GDP per capita to reflect grassroots usage and economic context.
- The Asia-Pacific region strengthened its apparent lead in the adoption of crypto assets.
- North America became the region of second-highest adoption driven by accelerated participation of traditional financial firms, according to Chainalysis research.
- Results from Chainalysis should be interpreted with caution, as they rely on web traffic estimates and frequently updated methodologies. Further analysis may be necessary to validate findings and account for these limitations.

### 5. Growth in Crypto Markets by Region

Percent change in total value received by centralized and decentralized services, year ended June 2025 vs. year ended June 2024

- Nearly every region saw higher growth in 2025 relative to 2024. Asia Pacific is the fastest growing region for on-chain crypto activity, with around 70 percent Y-o-Y growth in value received by centralized and decentralized services.
- Crypto activity rose 63 percent in Latin America and 52 percent in sub-Saharan Africa, likely driven by growing retail and institutional use, and continued reliance on crypto for remittances, according to [2025 Chainalysis Adoption Index](https://www.chainalysis.com/) .

Crypto markets continued to grow, led by Asia Pacific and Latin America.
